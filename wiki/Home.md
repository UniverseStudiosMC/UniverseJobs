# UniverseJobs Wiki

Welcome to the official UniverseJobs documentation! UniverseJobs is the ultimate next-generation jobs plugin for Minecraft servers.

## Features

- **20+ Action Types** - Break, Place, Kill, Harvest, Craft, Fish, Enchant, Brew, and more
- **Advanced Condition System** - Biome, Permission, Placeholder, Time, Weather, World conditions
- **Complete Reward System** - Items, money, commands with cooldowns
- **Boost & Multiplier System** - XP and Money multipliers with multiple calculation modes
- **11 Plugin Integrations** - Nexo, ItemsAdder, Oraxen, MythicMobs, mcMMO, and more
- **Leaderboards** - Global and per-job rankings with async database support
- **Full GUI System** - Customizable menus with pagination
- **Paper & Folia Compatible** - Full async support for modern servers
- **Database Support** - MySQL and SQLite with HikariCP connection pooling

## Requirements

- **Java 21** or higher
- **Paper 1.13+** or **Folia**
- **Vault** (for economy features)
- **PlaceholderAPI** (optional, for placeholders)
